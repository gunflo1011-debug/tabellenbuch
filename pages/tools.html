Interactive mathematics

Learn by trying it.

These tools are built into the reference itself. Calculate, experiment and then jump straight into the mathematics behind the result.

Algebra

Solve and explore equations.

Linear equation solver

Solve equations of the form ax + b = 0 and understand the special cases.

Quadratic equation solver

Find real or complex roots and inspect the discriminant of ax² + bx + c = 0.

2×2 linear-system solver

Solve two simultaneous linear equations and connect the result to matrix form and geometry.

Calculus

See change, accumulation and convergence.

Derivative & tangent tool

Differentiate a cubic polynomial, evaluate the slope at x₀ and generate the tangent line.

Extrema analyzer

Inspect critical points of cubic functions and connect first and second derivatives to graph shape.

Integral & Riemann-sum tool

Compute a definite polynomial integral and compare it with a midpoint-rectangle approximation.

Partial-sum explorer

Watch geometric, p-series and alternating harmonic partial sums approach — or fail to approach — a limit.

Multivariable & vector calculus

Explore mathematics in more dimensions.

Gradient analyzer

Evaluate a quadratic surface gradient, its magnitude and the direction of steepest ascent.

Vector-field diagnostic

Explore divergence and curl for a linear two-dimensional vector field.
